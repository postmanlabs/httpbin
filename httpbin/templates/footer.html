Proxy headers

httpbin.org sits behind proxies, so /headers and the headers field of every response hide the headers they add (X-Forwarded-For, X-Real-Ip, Via and similar). Add the query parameter show_env (?show_env=1, any value) to see them: /headers?show_env=1. It also shows cookies that are hidden by default.
